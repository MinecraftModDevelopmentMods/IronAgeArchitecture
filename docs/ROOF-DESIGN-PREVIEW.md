# Roof Design Preview

This is a local 0.1.16.110021 preview for trying the workbench and clipboard changes.
The player JAR and save are supplied separately from the earlier workbench preview.

## What to try

Start at the thatched house with the gutter along its eave. Its full attic floor sits
inside the surrounding wall course. The gabled dormer and rooflight belong to its
roof drawing. The next house has a raised slate roof and an open vault. The clay
roof has a wide shed dormer and a mansard on both sides. The next thatched house
has a mansard on one side and a two-high casement. The last house retains the first
workbench's roof profile for comparison.

Use the bench to turn on the personal ghost. Its roof page controls floor position,
collar clearance, eave drop, framing and the edge finish. Select each of the four
sides to adjust its reach. Raising the roof leaves the floor in place.

Take the clipboard from the separate slot, or use one on the bench to link it.
Sneak-use in the air to open its controls. Choose a tool and point at the ghost or
finished roof. Select a feature by sneak-using it, or choose it with Previous/Next.
Try changing its timber, leading, wall finish, width and covering. Undo and redo
change the drawing; they do not alter the house until you return to build.

At the bench, the bill lists additions, reuse and returns. Try a local dormer edit
with an already covered roof. An unchanged section should keep its covering and
window position. Survival needs the listed materials; Creative can build freely.
Main roof coverings are fitted by hand, while dormer features include theirs.

Open the rooflight and both sections of the tall casement. Try blocking a swing,
then removing the obstruction. Matching vertical sections operate together, while
neighbouring columns remain independent. Redstone changes operate the windows;
a hand operation lasts until the next change in power.

## Scope of this preview

The floor outline is rectangular, with a 32 by 32 bound and a 1,024-cell roof limit.
Separate roof drawings are still needed for larger or irregular buildings. Main
gable infill and walls beneath a raised roof remain ordinary building work. The
sample gable ends are partly open so the framing can be inspected. Roof covering
is limited to the enabled thatch, slate and clay families. Pipe routing remains
manual. Match a rooflight's Roof option to its covering for the right reveal depth.

Automated checks, native captures and performance measurements are recorded in
the candidate's qualification notes. Those checks do not replace a gameplay review.
The build has not been pushed, merged or published.
