0.1.10.110021 - Alpha

I've added the clay tiles and timber roof trim I've been trying out on the Rye cottages.

- Build with weathered clay tile slopes and matching ridge caps. The slopes turn hips
  and valleys like the existing roofs.
- Hang the same tiles on gables and upper walls. Thin panels line up with half-depth
  timber walls and leaded windows.
- Finish straight eaves with dark oak fascia boards. Matching verge and ridge end pieces
  give a projecting gable a continuous timber edge and a thin underside.
- Start with bricks and sticks, then turn the slopes into ridge caps, trim or hanging
  panels. The clay pieces are in the Late Medieval & Tudor palette.

0.1.8.110021 - Alpha

This update adds openable leaded windows and tidies up the timber framing. The village
kit is easier to craft, and you can now choose which building eras to use in survival.

- Added diamond and rectangular leaded windows in all six woods, with raw or limewashed
  surrounds. Full and half-depth frames fit ordinary walls and projecting upper floors.
- Stack matching sections for a taller window. Each column swings outwards on its own
  with a right-click or redstone. Leave room outside for the pane to swing.
- Rails now meet the posts and corners around windows, and diagonal braces keep their
  width where they cross between blocks. Fixed stray rail ends and flickering timber.
- Added a beam above lower walls to close the gap beneath the floorboards. The space
  under the overhang stays open. The diagonal brackets now have solid sides and ends.
- Split the kit into Iron Age and Late Medieval & Tudor creative tabs. Pick the eras
  you want to craft in the config. Cob, plain daub and thatch are shared by both.
- Start with plain wattle and daub and shape matching post panels into the patterns
  you need. Full, thin and short walls use the same recipes. Offcuts can be rejoined,
  paired patterns swap one-for-one, and tooltips give you a reminder while you build.
- Expanded limewashing to cover cob stairs and the other raw pieces, one at a time or
  in batches of eight. Cob stairs now give eight from six cob, and roof ridges can be
  turned back into slopes.


0.1.2.110021 - First alpha

This is the first building kit for Iron Age Architecture, starting on Minecraft 1.10.2.

- Added raw and limewashed cob blocks, slabs and stairs.
- Added wattle-and-daub walls in all six vanilla woods, with plain infill, posts,
  rails, close studding, diagonal braces and matching corner and end pieces.
- Added thin walls and short returns for building in half-block steps.
- Added timber floors with exposed joists, joined edge beams and optional brackets.
  Upper floors can project by half a block or a whole block, including around corners
  and across several storeys.
- Added smooth thatch and slate roofs, automatic roof corners and separate ridge caps.
- Added crafting recipes using vanilla materials and configuration switches for the
  main content families.

Iron Age Architecture works on its own. Iron Age Furniture is optional.

This is an early alpha. Feedback on the appearance and placement of the pieces is welcome.
