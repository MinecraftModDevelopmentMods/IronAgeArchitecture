0.1.8.110021 - Horizontal rails (unreleased)

- Joined rails through posts to matching corners and other timbers, closing the gaps
  between window framing and the corner posts.
- Stopped corner pieces adding horizontal returns on an unconnected side.
- Trimmed the backs of corner rails to the inside post so their cut ends do not show
  through the adjoining wall.
- Added close-up exterior and interior views to the new Horizontal Rails Preview.


0.1.7.110021 - Timber joins (unreleased)

- Fixed short rail ends sticking out beyond the posts or studs they should meet.
- Made automatic rails finish on diagonal braces at the right height, including thin
  walls and short pieces. Continuous rail runs keep their full width.
- Filled the small missing corners where diagonal beams cross between blocks, so
  the timber keeps its width through the joint. The joins follow the neighbouring wood.
- Corrected the diagonal bracing on half-step upper floors in the preview street.
  The new Timber Joins Preview keeps the earlier saves available for comparison.


0.1.6.110021 - Leaded windows (unreleased)

- Added openable leaded windows to the Late Medieval & Tudor palette, with diamond
  or rectangular leading, all six woods, and raw or limewashed surrounds.
- Added full and half-depth frames that fit the existing walls and jettied upper floors.
  Stack matching sections for taller casements, or place columns side by side.
- Casements swing outwards. Right-click the frame or open pane to operate a column;
  redstone works too. Neighbouring columns can be opened separately.
- Windows check the whole column before opening, so an obstruction leaves it closed.
- Craft leaded panes from glass panes and iron, then add one to a matching post panel.
  Shape the panels before glazing. Leading swaps one-for-one, and raw surrounds can
  be limewashed singly or in batches.


0.1.5.110021 - Crafting (unreleased)

- Made plain wattle and daub the starting material for timber wall panels.
- Gave full, thin and short walls the same pattern recipes. Arrange three post panels
  in a row, column, diagonal or L to make rails, studs, braces or corner/end panels.
- Made paired designs swap one-for-one without extra sticks. Patterned wall panels
  can be reshaped back into post panels.
- Added the missing ways to rejoin thin walls and recover shaped corner pieces.
- Added single and batch limewashing for every raw cob and daub shape, including stairs.
- Added crafting hints to the building pieces and rewrote the crafting guide around the new chains.
- Made brackets from matching timber floors and made roof ridges reversible.
- Increased the cob stair recipe to eight stairs and added recovery recipes so shaping
  does not waste material.

0.1.2.110021 - First alpha (unreleased)

This is the first building kit for Iron Age Architecture, starting on Minecraft 1.10.2.

- Added raw and limewashed cob blocks, slabs and stairs.
- Added wattle-and-daub walls in all six vanilla woods, with plain infill, posts,
  rails, close studding, diagonal braces and matching corner and end pieces.
- Added thin walls and short returns for building in half-block steps.
- Added timber floors with exposed joists, joined edge beams and optional brackets.
  Upper floors can project by half a block or a whole block, including around corners
  and across several storeys.
- Added smooth thatch and slate roofs, automatic roof corners and separate ridge caps.
- Added crafting recipes using vanilla materials and configuration switches for the
  main content families.

Iron Age Architecture works on its own. Iron Age Furniture is optional.

This is an early alpha. Feedback on the appearance and placement of the pieces is welcome.
